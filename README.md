## 🌍 Overview :
This dataset contains over 530,000 sales transactions across multiple countries. It includes details on products, prices, quantities, customers, and transaction dates, making it useful for analyzing customer behavior, sales trends, and product performance.

## 🎯 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) :
- 📈 Revenue Trends Throughout the Year – Understand seasonal and monthly sales performance.
- 🏆 Top-Selling Products Analysis – Identify best-performing products by sales.
- ⏳ Sales Trends Over Time – Track overall demand and customer purchasing behavior.
- 🔄 Repeat vs One-Time Customers – Compare loyal vs. one-time buyers.
- 💰 Top 15 Customers by Revenue in UK – Highlight high-value customers in the UK market.
- 🛒 Purchase Frequency – Measure unique transactions per customer.

---

## 💡Final Insights :
1. A large share of revenue is driven by a small set of top products and loyal customers.Seasonal peaks exist,indicating high-demand periods (likely holidays or festivals).
2. Certain products consistently dominate sales, making them crucial for stock planning and promotions.Low-selling products may be tying up resources.
3. Repeat customers generate significantly higher revenue compared to one-time buyers.The UK contributes the most to sales, showing market familiarity and loyalty.
4. Popular products tend to be in the low-to-mid price range but sell in higher quantities.Premium products sell less frequently but bring higher margin per unit.
5. Daily/Monthly sales trends show fluctuations with noticeable spikes (holiday seasons).Weekday vs weekend differences suggest promotions should be timed strategically.
6. Revenue is strongly driven by quantity, but also influenced by pricing strategies.Discounts and bulk purchases likely fuel high-volume sales.

---

## ✅ Recommendation :
1. Keep higher stock levels for the top-selling products during peak months.Reduce or phase out persistently low-performing items.
2. Introduce loyalty rewards, referral benefits, or subscription models for repeat buyers.Personalize offers for high-value customers.
3. Focus discounts on bulk purchases of mid-range products to drive volume.Premium items can be marketed with exclusivity rather than discounts.
4. Since the UK dominates sales, explore scaling into similar English-speaking or culturally aligned markets (e.g., Ireland, Canada, Australia).
5. Run targeted campaigns during festive/holiday seasons when sales spike.Off-season bundles and promotions can stabilize revenue.
6. Implement real-time dashboards for tracking product performance and revenue by customer.Set up anomaly detection to catch unusual dips or spikes quickly.
